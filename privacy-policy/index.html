1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Football Live Score: MatchLive ("MatchLive", "we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the application. We aim to provide football scores, fixtures, results, statistics, standings, and related information while respecting your privacy.

By using the app, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

2. Information and app activity

The app may process information needed to provide its features, depending on the version, device settings, and services enabled:

  • App and device information: technical information such as app version, device type, operating system, language, and diagnostic or performance data, where collected by the app or service providers.
  • Usage information: interactions with app features, viewed matches or competitions, and app performance events, where analytics tools are enabled.

We do not ask you to provide a name, phone number, or account profile simply to browse football information unless a particular feature clearly requires it.

Information may be used to operate and maintain the app, retrieve and display football data, improve reliability and performance, understand feature usage, diagnose errors, respond to support requests, and help protect the app against misuse.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers supporting the app, disclosed when required by law, or used to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
